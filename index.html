RunXRoam Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026 · Version 2026-10-03

Your information, explained simply

RunXRoam brings together your activities, progress, events, and community. We save the information you give us and the activities you choose to import. Other members can see information you share. You can change activity visibility, disconnect Strava, and ask us to delete your information.

For younger athletes: read this with a parent or guardian. Keep your birthday private, avoid posting your home or school address, and ask a trusted adult before sharing photos or meeting people from the app. If something worries you, use report or block, or email us.

1. Who is responsible

Pixel Nomads, based in Marigondon, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu, Philippines, operates RunXRoam and is the personal information controller for information handled through the app. Contact our privacy team at contact@pixelnomads.app.

This policy covers our web and mobile services. Features may vary by platform. It explains our processing under applicable privacy law, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012. External services have their own policies.

2. Information we handle

  • Accounts: email address, account identifier, sign-in provider, verification status, and account authentication information. Google or Apple sign-in, where available, supplies the identity details you authorize. Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication.
  • Profiles: display name, biography, profile photo, location, country, team, preferred activities, appearance settings, featured badges, and privacy preferences. Date of birth is requested when saving profile information and is used for age-related features. Its visibility is separate from age-group display.
  • Activities: activity type, title, date and start time, duration, distance, elevation, calories where supplied, source, external activity identifier, import time, notes, and audience. Activities can come from manual entry, supported GPX, TCX, FIT or CSV files, and authorized Strava imports. We derive progress totals, pace, personal records, streaks, goals, and achievements from these records. A selected source file may contain more information than the fields RunXRoam saves.
  • Events and clubs: saved participation choices, selected distances, linked completion activities, event submissions, club memberships and roles, group events, and race or virtual-challenge tracking. Browsing and filtering use the location, preferences, or search input relevant to the feature.
  • Community: shared activities and achievements, follows and follow requests, reactions, comments, reports, blocks, and in-app notification preferences and read status.
  • Rankings: submitted race results, distances and finish times, evidence links, review decisions, ranking preferences, age groups, calculated performance information, and optional ITRA profile links and retrieved public runner statistics.
  • Support payments: the selected support tier, amount, account identifier, timestamp, and derived supporter status. The current GCash flow is manual; an app record or badge is not proof that GCash confirmed payment. Payment credentials, PINs and one-time passwords belong in GCash, not RunXRoam.
  • Service operation and support: the messages and attachments you send, consent document versions and acceptance times, and request, error, security and operational records. Hosting and infrastructure providers receive connection information such as IP addresses when handling requests.

3. Location and device access

RunXRoam aggregates activities; it does not currently record live GPS workouts or continuously track you in the background. When you choose “Near Me,” it requests foreground location access to obtain your current coordinates for nearby results. You can deny access and use other location filters. This does not remove locations or dates you have already entered or shared.

Maps and place searches process locations, search text, event coordinates and related requests. Opening external directions passes the destination to the selected maps service. Profile locations, activity notes, event attendance and photos can also reveal where you live or spend time.

Photo and file selection lets you supply profile media or import activities. Downloading a share image may use your device's media-library permission. Calendar access is used when you ask to add an event; the feature does not upload your existing calendar entries. Permissions and available controls differ between browsers, Android and iOS. You can change permissions in device or browser settings.

4. Why we use information

We use account and profile data to provide access and personalize your profile; activity data to organize your history and calculate progress; event and club information to manage your selections and memberships; and shared content to provide community features. Results and evidence support ranking calculations and review. Support records enable supporter recognition. Correspondence and operational information help us answer requests, investigate problems, moderate abuse and protect the service.

For ordinary personal information, the applicable basis depends on the purpose: providing the service you request, complying with law, your consent, or legitimate interests such as service security after considering your rights. For sensitive information, including age and health-related information, an applicable sensitive-information ground is required; ordinary legitimate interests alone are insufficient. Where consent is the basis, it must be specific and may be withdrawn by using the relevant control or contacting us. Accepting the Terms is not blanket consent to unrelated processing.

Statistics, achievements and rankings are informational calculations, can contain errors, and are not medical assessments. We do not use your activity information to make decisions about employment, credit or insurance.

5. Visibility and sharing

Core profiles are discoverable by signed-in members. Activity audiences are “Everyone,” “Connections,” and “Only me.” The current default for new profiles is Connections; in the social system this means approved followers. Badge visibility defaults to Everyone, and achievement posting defaults to asking first. Existing accounts and individual activities may have different saved settings. Review each audience before sharing.

Date of birth is hidden by default and has a separate visibility setting. An age group may still appear in relevant ranking features. ITRA links have their own visibility setting. Hiding a link does not make the underlying ITRA public record or shared cache private. Ranking leaderboard participation and activity privacy are separate controls.

Community content, club membership and event attendance can be visible to the audience of the relevant feature. Administrators can access information needed for support, moderation, submission review and service administration. A hidden field in a screen is not necessarily a private record; contact us with questions about particular data or to request restricted processing.

Changing an audience cannot remove screenshots, downloaded share images or copies already held outside RunXRoam. External registration links take you to the organizer or registration service, which handles information you provide under its own terms. Selecting Interested, Going or Completed in RunXRoam does not itself register you with an organizer.

6. Connected services and recipients

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: account authentication, database storage and synchronization, uploaded profile media, backend processing and operational logging. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Strava: optional OAuth connection for one-way activity import. The requested activity-reading permission can include private Strava activities you authorize. We store connection identifiers and access/refresh tokens on the backend to maintain the connection. Your RunXRoam audience settings apply to imported records; do not assume a Strava privacy setting automatically carries over. Disconnect in RunXRoam or revoke access in Strava. Disconnecting does not by itself delete existing imports, and deleting a RunXRoam record does not delete the original from Strava. See Strava's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps Platform and external maps apps: map display, place searches, location lookup, nearby trip suggestions and directions. Requests can include the location or search input used by the feature. Google Maps use is also subject to Google Maps terms and Google's privacy policy.
  • ITRA and Apify: optional runner matching and retrieval of public ITRA statistics. The runner name and optional nationality used for a search are sent through Apify. A confirmed runner identifier and retrieved public statistics are cached. This does not require your ITRA password. See Apify's Privacy Policy and ITRA.
  • Google Cloud Vision: extraction of event-price text from event-source images when that enrichment feature runs. This is separate from personal activity screenshot import, which is not currently available.
  • GCash: voluntary support is sent through its separate app or QR flow. We do not receive your GCash PIN or login credentials. See GCash's Privacy Notice.
  • Apple, Google, GitHub and external sites: optional sign-in providers, platform services, and hosting or delivery of our public legal pages receive the requests needed to provide their services. Opening a link or loading content directly from another provider can expose your IP address and request information to that provider.

We may disclose relevant information when required by law or necessary and lawful to address abuse, protect people, or establish or defend legal claims. We do not sell personal information. A new unrelated use or new data-sharing purpose requires an updated disclosure and any consent required by law.

7. Storage, security and retention

Account-linked information is stored in cloud systems. Device or browser storage also supports sessions, preferences and caches. Infrastructure providers may process information outside the Philippines. We remain responsible for information under our control and use access controls and provider arrangements to protect it. No service can guarantee absolute security; legally required breach notifications will be provided.

We retain profiles, activity history and associated settings while needed to provide your account and requested features, or until you delete them or request erasure. Connection tokens are needed while a connection remains active. Community content is kept while needed for its publication and related interactions. Support, security and payment-related records may need retention to resolve an open request, investigate an incident, meet a legal recordkeeping obligation, or handle a claim. We assess those purposes when you request deletion and explain any specific exception rather than treating it as permission to keep everything indefinitely.

Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove restricted infrastructure logs or provider recovery copies. We do not promise a fixed backup-expiry period here because retention depends on the relevant provider and configured service. Ask us for the retention details applicable to your request. Retained copies must not be used to restore a deleted public profile or resume unrelated processing.

8. Deletion and privacy requests

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, type Delete in the confirmation text box, then choose Permanently Delete Account. You can also request deletion without opening or installing the app by emailing contact@pixelnomads.app with the subject “RunXRoam Account Deletion.” Identify your account and whether you want complete account-and-data deletion; never send your password. Uninstalling the app is not account deletion.

Self-service deletion removes the account and the data categories covered by the current deletion workflow, including the main profile, activity library and published activities, relevant social interactions, non-owner memberships, event tracking and submissions, feedback, profile uploads and the stored Strava connection. It is not a guarantee that every separately stored record is automatically removed. Birthday records, rankings, linked ITRA data, supporter records, reports and owned-club records may require additional review and cleanup; include them in your request for complete deletion. This implementation limitation does not reduce your right to request erasure.

If you own a club, transfer ownership or contact us about closing it and removing your identifying information. Public event facts supplied by multiple sources and records independently held by organizers or connected services may remain with those providers. We explain any information that must lawfully be retained and the reason.

To request access, correction, erasure, blocking, objection, applicable portability, or withdrawal of consent, email contact@pixelnomads.app. We may verify ownership using proportionate information; never send a password or one-time code. We will explain the scope, expected handling time, completion or any lawful limitation. You may complain to the Philippine National Privacy Commission, and seek remedies available under applicable law.

9. Younger users and families

RunXRoam is for athletes of all ages, including younger athletes with appropriate parent or legal-guardian involvement. If you are under 18, ask your parent or guardian to read these documents with you, supervise your use and approve sharing and payments. Where applicable law requires verified parental authorization before collecting a child's information, do not submit that information until the required authorization has been arranged with us. Contact us before creating an account if this applies to your child.

The current app does not provide a verified parental-consent workflow or a dedicated child account with separate privacy defaults. A standard legal-agreement checkbox does not verify parental permission. Families should use private activity settings, keep birthdays hidden, avoid identifying home or school details, and review club and event audiences. These choices do not replace our legal obligations.

A parent or guardian can contact us to request information about a child's account, correction, restricted processing or deletion, subject to appropriate verification and the child's applicable rights. Report suspected unauthorized collection or unsafe contact so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
